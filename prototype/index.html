02About me

Read Full Story / >

When I’m deeply involved in solving a problem or shaping a new product, I completely lose track of time. I believe having a career you genuinely enjoy is one of life’s greatest privileges. Throughout my career, I’ve helped design products used by millions of people across finance, business platforms, social apps, dashboards, robotics, infrastructure, and e-commerce.

I see myself as a builder—someone who turns ideas into real products.

By combining design with coding, I turn complex ideas into simple, useful, and scalable digital products—balancing a developer’s mindset, a user’s perspective, and real business needs.

I see AI less as a feature and more as a creative partner—something I think with, build with, and learn from. The best results come from a real conversation: curious, unexpected, and genuinely useful.
